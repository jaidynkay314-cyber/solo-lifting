Solo Lifting privacy

Solo Lifting is a personal, non-commercial gym and nutrition tracker used by a small group of friends. It is run by Jaidyn Kay. Last updated 3 November 2026.

What's stored where

  • On your phone only: body weight, measurements, progress photos, and any Oura data (readiness, sleep, heart rate, activity). These are never uploaded to Solo Lifting's server.
  • On the shared board (Supabase): your display name, workouts, food log totals and goals, so the group can see each other's progress. Lift weights are hidden from others unless you choose to show them.

Oura

If you connect Oura, the app asks Oura for read-only access to the data types you approve. The access token and the data it fetches stay in your phone's browser storage and are only used to show your readiness, sleep and activity inside the app. Nothing from Oura is shared with other users, sold, or used to train any model. You can disconnect at any time in the app, and revoke access from your Oura account.

No tracking

There are no ads, analytics or third-party trackers.

Deleting your data

Clear the app's data on your phone to remove everything stored there. Ask Jaidyn to remove your entries from the shared board.
